User Data Deletion Policy

Under the Google Play Store User Data Deletion Policy, users of Ama Sinapali Local Mart have the right to request permanent deletion of their account and associated data without requiring the app to be installed.

Data Types That Will Be Deleted

  • Account Credentials: Mobile phone number, verified email, and profile name.
  • Addresses & Geolocation: Delivery addresses, Odisha GP, block, village, and landmark notes.
  • Shopping Data: Active cart items, product wishlists, customer notifications.
  • Uploaded Media: Customer profile picture (DP), shop storefront photos, product photos.
  • Cloud Backend Records: Corresponding documents in Firebase Firestore collections (users, shops, products).

Data Retention Exceptions

Financial records, Cash on Delivery receipts, and completed historical purchase tallies are anonymized and retained solely for mandatory statutory accounting, tax (GST), and audit compliance under Indian law.

In-App Deletion Alternative: You can also delete your account instantly from within the Android app:
• For Customers: My Profile > Delete Account & Wipe Data
• For Shop Owners: Shop Dashboard > Delete Shop Account & Wipe Cloud Data
