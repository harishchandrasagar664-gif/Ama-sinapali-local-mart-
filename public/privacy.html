Ama Sinapali Local Mart

Privacy Policy & Data Safety Disclosures

Last Updated: October 2026 Effective Date: October 8, 2026 Jurisdiction: Sinapali, Nuapada, Odisha, India Developer: Ama Sinapali Local Mart Team

Welcome to Ama Sinapali Local Mart ("we", "our", or "the App"), a hyper-local e-commerce and delivery platform dedicated to connecting local retail shops, kirana merchants, vegetable/grocery vendors, and consumers across Sinapali Block and neighboring Gram Panchayats in Nuapada District, Odisha.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, handle, store, protect, and delete user personal and merchant data in compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies (including the User Data and Data Safety guidelines), the Information Technology Act, 2000 (India), and applicable consumer data protection standards.

1. Personal Data We Collect

A. Customer Account & Order Information

When you register as a customer or place orders through the App, we collect:

  • Contact Identifiers: Mobile phone number or email address (verified via OTP) used for login credentials, order status notifications, and customer support.
  • User Full Name: Provided during account creation to verify order deliveries.
  • Delivery Addresses: Local residential or delivery location within Sinapali, Nuapada, including Village/Town, Gram Panchayat (GP), Ward/Street, and landmarks.
  • Profile Photo (Optional): Optional display photo uploaded by you using the camera or system photo selector.

B. Shop Owner & Merchant Data

For local shop merchants operating storefronts on the platform, we collect:

  • Merchant Profile: Shop Owner Name, registered phone number, email, and password credentials.
  • Shop Details: Shop Name, physical retail address in Sinapali, category (Kirana, Grocery, Electronics, Clothing, etc.), opening/closing timings, and storefront photos.
  • Catalog & Inventory Data: Product titles, Odia translations, retail pricing, unit measurements (kg, gram, packet, litre), stock status, and product images.

C. Order & Transaction Records

  • Order Details: Selected items, order quantities, subtotal amounts, timestamp of order placement, and delivery address.
  • Payment Preferences: Cash on Delivery (COD) payment status and record of completed payments. (Note: We do not collect or store debit/credit card numbers or net banking passwords).
  • Delivery Milestones: Real-time status flags (Order Placed, Preparing, Out for Delivery, Delivered).

2. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To process, pack, and deliver orders from local shops to your doorstep in Sinapali.
  • To enable direct merchant-to-customer communication regarding item substitutions or delivery updates.
  • To send operational transaction SMS, alerts, and notifications about order progress.
  • To prevent fraudulent orders, duplicate registrations, and platform abuse.
  • To fulfill statutory commercial accounting and tax compliance under Indian regulations.
Zero Data Selling Guarantee: We never sell, lease, rent, or trade your personal information, mobile numbers, delivery addresses, or purchase history to third-party advertising companies, data brokers, or marketing networks.

3. Data Storage, Architecture & Security

We implement modern security controls to protect your data:

  • On-Device Storage: Local caching and temporary cart items are securely managed in local application SQLite Room storage on your Android device.
  • Cloud Backend (Firebase Firestore): Cloud database synchronization is managed through Google Cloud / Firebase Firestore using TLS/HTTPS encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Strict Role-Based Access: Shop owners only view order information (customer name, items, delivery location) specifically assigned to their retail store. System administration is strictly restricted to designated platform administrators.

4. Camera, Photos & Device Permissions

In accordance with Google Play's principle of least privilege:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Requested at runtime only if you choose to snap a live photo for your customer profile avatar or shop storefront banner.
  • Photo Library Selection: We utilize the zero-permission Android System Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia). The App never requests broad storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).
  • Internet & Connectivity: Used to communicate with the cloud backend to retrieve live shop catalogs and place orders.

5. Data Retention & Preservation

  • Active Accounts: Profile details and addresses are stored while your account remains active so you can order without re-entering data.
  • Cart & Notifications: Active cart items and temporary notifications are retained until fulfilled or cleared.
  • Completed Transaction Records: Financial invoices and order tallies may be retained in anonymized format solely for mandatory tax, GST, and audit preservation as mandated by Indian law.
  • Deleted Data: Upon account deletion, personal identifiers, contact numbers, saved addresses, wishlists, and cloud profile documents are immediately and permanently erased.

6. Account Deletion & Data Wipe Rights (Google Play Compliant)

You have the absolute right to permanently delete your account and wipe all associated personal data anytime, both from within the App and via the web:

A. In-App Deletion:

  • For Customers: Navigate to My Profile > tap 'Delete Account & Wipe Data'.
  • For Shop Owners: Navigate to Shop Dashboard > tap 'Delete Shop Account & Wipe Cloud Data'.

B. External Web Deletion (Without Installing the App):

If you have uninstalled the app or prefer to request deletion online, you can submit an instant deletion request via our public deletion page:

Go to Account Deletion Request Page

7. Children's Privacy

Ama Sinapali Local Mart is intended for general audiences and adults able to make hyper-local purchases in Sinapali. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, please contact our Grievance Officer immediately for removal.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications in our features, operational workflows, or regulatory guidelines. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated Effective Date. Continued use of the platform after updates signifies acceptance of the revised terms.
